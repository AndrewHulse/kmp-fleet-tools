Crab-Angle Finder

Paste two field points, pick the direction the robot should face, and get the crab-drive angle (0° = forward, 180° = backward) needed to move from A to B while holding that heading.

Tip: paste A -> B into Point A and Point B fills itself.

World frame: +X = East (0°), +Y = North (90°), CCW positive — matches WPILib field coordinates.

Vector directions are exact; lengths are normalized for readability, not drawn to field scale.

How it's computed: travel bearing = atan2(ΔY, ΔX) in the world frame above. The crab-drive angle is the robot's chosen heading minus that bearing, normalized to −180°…180°. Because −180° and 180° describe the same physical direction, an equivalent unwrapped value (±360°) is shown next to the primary result — use whichever range your drive code expects. Example: Main-Main-1013 (33.359, 86.024) → Main-Main-1020 (35.486, 87.542) facing South gives −126°.
