Rotation diameter is the circle swept by the furthest corner of the speed 1 safety field about the robot centre. Each side is its base safety field plus half of the load overhang on that axis. Left and right use the width overhang, front and back use the length overhang. If the load is smaller than the robot, the overhang is 0.
